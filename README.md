# 🌍 IMARES — Multi-Solutions Platform for Community Empowerment

IMARES is a web-based platform designed to deliver diverse digital services to the people of Wolaita Sodo and surrounding regions. Built with a mobile-first approach and clean UI, it offers tutoring, job matching, delivery coordination, business consulting, and more — all under one responsive interface.

---

## 🚀 Live Site

🔗 [Visit IMARES.com](https://imares.com)

---

## 🎯 Core Services

- 📚 **IMARES-Tutoring** — Connects students with local tutors
- 💼 **IMARES-Job Match** — Links employers with job seekers
- 🚚 **IMARES-Delivery** — Facilitates local delivery services
- 🛒 **IMARES-Buyer & Seller Hub** — Marketplace for goods and services
- 🧠 **IMARES-Business Consulting** — Offers strategic support for entrepreneurs
- 🏠 **IMARES-Housing Match** *(Coming Soon)* — Connects renters and landlords
- 💳 **IMARES-Online Payments** *(Coming Soon)* — Enables cashless transactions
- 🧠 **IMARES-Innovation & Mindset Training** *(Coming Soon)* — Skill-building for professionals

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript (ES6+)**
- **Bootstrap 4.1.3** — Responsive layout
- **Font Awesome & Google Fonts** — Iconography and typography
- **Lazy loading** — Optimized image performance
- **Optional:** Firebase for authentication and data storage
